Where the expected chances come from

Expected chances over the whole match, built up step by step for the events you placed (chances that would follow from them are not added back in). Small figures: 95% intervals from the model's cluster-robust covariance (delta method), which carry the uncertainty in the fitted coefficients, not in the events you place.

Events

    Lower chart. Each team's extra expected chances so far, compared with the same two teams in a match where nothing has happened. A goal's effect is small per minute but lasts for the rest of the match, so the lines drift apart; the gap between solid and dashed is what restarts and momentum add or take away.

    What you are seeing. Each team's rate of chances (possessions that end in a shot) per minute, from the main model fitted on all 1,517 matches of the 2015/16 Premier League, La Liga, Serie A and Ligue 1. Curves are computed directly from the fitted coefficients for the events you place. Nothing is simulated and no event data are on this page.

    Solid vs dashed. Solid: full model. Dashed: the same match with restarts and momentum switched off: every chance and goal still counts, but as if it had happened more than 20 minutes earlier, so only the score, the clock, venue, strength and the team's level that day remain. The breakdown splits the gap into restarts (the first two minutes after an event) and momentum (2-20 minutes), the same split as in the analysis.

    Why a goal moves the curves for the rest of the match. The score changes: a trailing team creates about 13% more chances and a leading team about 15% fewer, conditional on strength and the clock. That is a description, not proof of tactics.

    Why curves dip right after an event. After a goal there is about a minute with no play (celebration, kick-off), and after a chance the ball is usually dead (goal kick, corner, keeper), while a rebound in the same attack is part of the same chance by definition.

    The small bump 2-20 minutes after a chance is the momentum effect. It came from an exploratory analysis, not a pre-registered one: about +0.05 chances in total per chance. Halves are drawn as 47 minutes; red cards are not part of this page.
